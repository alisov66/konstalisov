  - I increasingly use code and AI-assisted development to prototype ideas, understand implementation, and collaborate directly with engineering.

  - Sole Product Designer at MiLaboratories is good evidence of independent ownership.

- **Design systems — strong**
  - Real production experience with reusable components, patterns, documentation, consistency, and scalable foundations.
